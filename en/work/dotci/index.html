DOTCI

A web app for new students: arrive at a campus spot and a note from an older student opens

Open the appSee the proof

At a glance

What
A location-based web app where the place is the search term. I checked the system through QA, did the visual design, and presented with a 20-slide deck and script.
When
Fall semester 2026
My role
System QA, visual design, presentation (team of 3)
Why it matters
New students get to know a strange campus through notes left at each spot.
Standard
QA checks that the system follows its rules: notes open only where you are, and each note is 200 characters at most.
Output
A live web app. 21 campus spots, arrival checked by GPS or QR.
Status
Live. On-campus · 1st place — 2026 Creative Convergence Collaborative Learning Competition (Woosong University, 2026.11.06)
