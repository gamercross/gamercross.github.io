Floe

A desktop app that helps people who code with AI see it through to the end

See the proofReleases

At a glance

What
A desktop guide for vibe coding. As team lead I set the name and structure, split the schedule and roles, and cleared about 120 stalled decisions in one sweep.
When
Sep – Nov 2026
My role
Team lead, project lead, AI integration (team of 5)
Why it matters
Of 7 people who had tried AI coding, 5 gave up halfway (team survey of 8). Floe helps them finish. For me, it was how I came to understand software engineering.
Standard
Each step has a gate and a comprehension check before you move on · Reported problems go on one fix list and get fixed together
Output
Apps for Mac, Windows and Ubuntu, 15 releases
Status
