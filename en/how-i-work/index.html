Link small goals to big goals

Work manual v2 · Goal ladder

One page shows where this week's work lands in this month's, this quarter's and this year's goals. Fill the bottom row first, then link each item to the row above.

  • Items that don't reach a higher goal become “stretch candidates.” Two stretches at a time, at most.
  • Every Sunday night, AI drafts the goal page and I review and choose.
  • I don't backfill missed weeks. I note them on the next page.

I want to lay out what I'm doing this week, and my goals for this month, this quarter and this year … and turn it into a manual that starts from the small goals.

Split tasks: me, AI, others

Ontology v0.2 · What exists and how it connects

Each line in the log is one task. Each task has one owner and one state. The owner is me, AI, or someone else.

  • One task, one owner, one state. If there are two, split the task.
  • A waiting task links to what it waits on.
  • I always make the decisions.
  • Unblock links never form a loop.

I need to sort what AI does and what I have to do, and show the work split up with an ontology.

AI가 하는것들 내가 해야하는것들을 정리해서 온톨리지 시스템으로 작업한것들을 나눠서 보여줘야해

Sort urgent from important

Four boxes · Urgent? × Important?

I gather every open task in one place and sort it into four boxes. Important means it serves my priority list and 2027 goals. Urgent means it's due within 7 days or has a set time.

  • Box 1 holds three a day, at most. The rest go to box 2 and get a date.
  • Inside a box, what can't be missed comes first.
  • Box 4 is for things to drop. I choose what to drop, too.

I want to sort my tasks into the four boxes, urgent or not and important or not, so I can plan and invest my time in reaching my goals.

요구사항들을 내가 말했던 4가지로 급한가 중요한가로 해서 나누고 정리해서 내가 목적을 이루기 위해서 시간들을 정리하고 투자를 할 수 있도록 하고 싶어

Which tool does what

Channel map · This goes here

Once I had several places to talk to AI, the same work started happening in two places. So each kind of work now has one home. My part is to talk, choose, judge, and say “yes.”

  • I talk in one place: Orca.
  • Nothing is sent, submitted, or merged until I say yes.
  • All records live in one place: my-os.

… I should also be able to explain to other people how I use Orca and Aside here.

I draw each frame as one board

When I use these frames to plan, I draw them with archi-board, a Claude Code skill I made. It reads the code and docs and puts who uses it, what they get, and in what order on one board.

I studied software engineering with a mentor. When I set out to plan a system, I turned the methods I learned, and my way of drawing the work, into a Claude skill. This is that process.
